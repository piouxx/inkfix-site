How it works

You stay in control at every step. Your live store doesn't change until you press publish yourself.

  1. Free preview

    Send your store's address. I check five key pages, including your home page, a collection and a product, and email you the results and the price.

  2. You pick your colours

    A short sheet shows your accent colours as swatches. For each, choose "keep exactly" or "a slightly deeper shade is fine".

  3. Fixes go on a copy

    With collaborator access, I add the fixes to a duplicate, unpublished copy of your theme and test it there.

  4. You publish, I re-check

    You publish when you're happy. I test your live store again afterwards, then my access is removed.

What Inkfix changes, and what it doesn't

Changes

  • Text and buttons whose colours are too faint to read easily
  • Links a screen reader announces with no name, such as icon-only social and cart links
  • All inside your own theme: one small CSS file and a few link labels

Doesn't change

  • Your layout, fonts, photos or wording
  • Any accent colour you ask to keep exactly
  • Problems automated testing can't find. It catches some accessibility problems, not all, so Inkfix is not a full accessibility audit and makes no legal claims about your store.

Questions

Will my brand colours change?

Only if you say a slightly deeper shade is fine. If you don't answer, they stay exactly as they are.

Do you install an app or a script?

No. The fixes are plain CSS and link labels inside your own theme, so nothing slows your store down, and nothing breaks if you stop working with Inkfix.

What if I don't like the result?

Nothing goes live until you publish the theme copy yourself. If you don't like it, delete the copy and your store stays as it was.

What access do you need?

None for the free preview. To install, collaborator access to your Shopify admin, which you can remove at any time. I only work on the unpublished copy of your theme.

About InkfixAudit, our page checker

If you've seen "InkfixAudit" in your server logs, that's our checker loading your pages. It only loads public pages, at most five per store, no more than one page per second.
